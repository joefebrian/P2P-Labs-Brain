## Recommended starter test matrix for an AIOSCreator-type product
1. [[Styles/Problem - Solution|Problem / Solution]] 20–30 s × 3 hooks (callout, stat, 'AI slop' pain), the default
2. [[Styles/App Walkthrough|App Walkthrough]] 30 s × 3 hooks (outcome, 'here's my setup', how-to)
3. [[Styles/Comment Response|Comment Response]] × 3 objections ('is AI content generic?', 'is it hard to set up?', 'why local-first?')
4. [[Styles/Before & After|Before & After]] (manual scripting vs generated) and [[Styles/Skit|Skit]] (burnt-out vs chill affiliate)
5. Retarget with [[Styles/Testimonial|Testimonial]] (real users) and [[Styles/Motion Graphics|Motion Graphics]] (offer bumpers)
