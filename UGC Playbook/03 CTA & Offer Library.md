## How often offer devices appear (anywhere in transcript or on-screen text)
| Device | % of all analysed videos |
|---|---|
| urgency time | 21% |

| free shipping | 2% |
| promo code | 1% |

- 48% of speaking videos have an explicit verbal CTA in the last ~12 s. The rest rely on the platform's product anchor or button, an end card, or an implied CTA.
- The typical order is **proof → risk reversal → discount/gift → scarcity → action verb + place** (for example: *"30-day money back guarantee. Link below."*).
- Common action verbs: *grab, tap, click, try, shop, check out, head to*. Common places: *link below, in my bio, TikTok Shop, orange cart, brand.com*.

## CTA formulas
- `{Guarantee}, so you have nothing to lose. Tap the link below.`
- `Right now {offer}. {Scarcity}. Grab yours {place}.`
- `It's free for {N} days, so give it a shot.` (software)
- `Comment {KEYWORD} and I'll send you {resource}.` (lead magnet / DM automation)
- `{Character punchline}: tap below, unless you enjoy my company.` (AI-cartoon)
- `They have a money back guarantee, but you probably won't use it.` (skit close)
- `I earn a commission if you buy through my link.` (affiliate disclosure, required: see below)

## Offer stacks that recur in the data
- **Guarantee + % off + free gifts + selling out**: the AI-Cartoon and Everyday Dose / RYZE long advertorials.
- **First-order % off + free shaker/bottle**: Oats Overnight street interviews and B-roll.
- **BOGO / buy-2-get-2**: The Foldie, BURGA, HeyShape.
- **Risk-free N days/nights**: The Foldie (60 days), Loop (100 nights), Seed/Onnit/RYZE (30 days), Mars Men (90 days).
- **Free trial**: Motion ('free for 7 days'), Linktree ('7-day free trial'), Canva ('sign up today for free').

## Compliance (web guidance; see [[01 How to Write a UGC Script]])
- Affiliate or paid relationships must be disclosed **in the video** (spoken and/or on-screen), not only in the caption or bio. The FTC says a disclosure in a TikTok text description alone is unlikely to be clear and conspicuous. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking and https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers
- Only state offers that are live and true (discount, deadline, stock). Never fake scarcity.
