# Unboxing

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
First-person opening of the package: packaging, contents, first impressions, first use, verdict. Often with bonus or free-gift reveals.

## When to use it
Use it for physical products with premium packaging, bundles, free gifts, and subscription boxes. For software, 'unbox' a new feature or template pack as a digital unboxing.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **anticipation** | 0–3s | The package in hand plus the hook |
| 2 | **reveal** | 3–15s | Item by item |
| 3 | **first impression** | 15–25s | Texture, quality, details |
| 4 | **first use** | 25–40s | Try it |
| 5 | **verdict + offer** | last 5s | Bonus and CTA |

## Hook formulas
- `I've got {N} free gifts with this {kit} and I'm going to show you exactly what's inside.`
- `It finally came!`
- `Unboxing the {viral product} everyone's been talking about.`
- `Let's see if {product} is worth the hype.`

## Script structure as observed
arrival hook (viral/just launched/finally arrived) -> layer-by-layer reveal (ASMR) -> item callouts + features -> first use -> verdict -> offer ($150 off). Median ~34s.

## CTA patterns observed
- "You can get $150 off your cookware set today" (Pretty Twinkle Design 11)
- "Shop now and get yours today!" (Caraway 20)
- "Now the link is down below. If you want to give it a try, they do a 30-day guarantee" (RYZE 14)

## What the strongest examples do
- Anticipation, then item-by-item reveals, gives a natural pacing structure.
- Free gifts or bundles are revealed on camera, so the offer is visible.
- First use happens on camera, so the viewer gets the verdict immediately.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** big branded box on the floor/kitchen island (Caraway dominant, 10 of 20), hands opening layers, text "Unbox My New Caraway Cookware Set With Me", "Caraway just launched their new Prep Set" + knife reveal, body pillow unboxing on bed (Buffy 15), SheCurve package on bed, RYZE Premium Starter Kit with mug/frother, cooking first use after unboxing. Text pills in TikTok-native white boxes. 35% music-only ASMR style.

**Spec:** 9:16. Top-down or selfie; hands-in-frame; crisp ASMR audio; one item per shot. See the style statistics for the length.
