# AI - Cartoon Style

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
Fully AI-generated 3D/Pixar-like animation. The problem itself is often a character (belly fat, a gut, a stressed brain), and a narrator voices it in a long direct-response advertorial.

## When to use it
Use it when the problem is invisible or internal (gut, stress, time lost, messy workflow), when you have no on-camera talent, or when you want scroll-stopping novelty at scale. It fits the Characters and ShortDrama modules. Avoid it for products that need real-person trust as proof.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **hook** | 0–3s | A personified problem speaks in first person, or 'Here's what happens to a [age] [person] after [N] days of [habit]' |
| 2 | **agitate** | 3–20s | Symptoms and consequences, visualised inside the body or system; 'it's not your fault' |
| 3 | **mechanism** | 20–35s | The real cause, explained simply with an animated metaphor |
| 4 | **reveal** | ~40–55% of runtime | Product enters as the fix; name it and show the pack or UI |
| 5 | **how it works** | +10–20s | Ingredients or features mapped one by one to each symptom |
| 6 | **timeline/proof** | +10s | 'By week 1… by day 30…', reviews, celebrity or authority |
| 7 | **offer stack** | last 15s | Guarantee + % off + free gifts + scarcity |
| 8 | **cta** | last 3–5s | 'Tap the link below…' often voiced by the character |

## Hook formulas
- `I'm the {problem} you've been trying to get rid of for {time}, and I need to tell you something nobody else will.`
- `Here's what happens to a {age}-year-old {persona} after {N} days of {new habit}.`
- `If your {symptom 1}, {symptom 2} and {symptom 3}, then listen up, because {hidden cause}.`
- `You're not {lazy/bad at X}. {Real cause}.`
- `Officials are asking the public to remain calm. (news parody)`

## Script structure as observed
long (median ~64s, up to 294s), long-form direct-response advertorial: symptom callout -> mechanism "what's really happening inside" (visualised) -> "it's not your fault" -> product reveal around the midpoint (brand median ~51% of runtime) -> ingredient/mechanism -> timeline "By week 1..." -> social proof (reviews, David Beckham) -> offer stack (money-back guarantee, % off, free gifts, sells out) -> "link below".

## CTA patterns observed
- "Tap the link below and finally get rid of me." (Obvi 17)
- "They sell out constantly, so grab it while it's here." (Everyday Dose 20)
- "Tap below, unless you enjoy my company." (RYZE 30)

## What the strongest examples do
- The problem is a character with a voice and a personality. That creates curiosity and humour and makes the ad feel less like one.
- Every symptom gets its own visual metaphor, so the mechanism is *seen*, not just heard.
- Long runtime (median ~64 s) is earned: the reveal sits near the midpoint, then an offer stack closes (guarantee + % off + gifts + scarcity).

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** Pixar-like 3D AI renders; personified organs/fat/poop characters (belly-fat blob, angry pouch, skeleton), a cartoon doctor next to a realistic guy, a gut cross-section with glowing inflammation, a "travel poop weight" game UI, a cartoon version of a real man on a beach. Bold white/yellow caption word-by-word, centered mid-frame; some red-box headline text. Some carry "AI generated video/performer" disclosure. 9:16 (3 are 4:5).

**Spec:** 9:16 at 1080×1920. Consistent stylised 3D character (Characters module); scenes change every 2–4 s (study median about 3.3 cuts per 10 s); bold word-by-word captions mid-frame; an 'AI-generated' label where required. VO is fast (median about 195 wpm while speaking). Long runtime: median about 64 s, and many run 55–90 s.

**Categories seen:** supplements, gut health, mushroom coffee, pet health (PetLab), weight loss, shapewear, soap.

```text
[50–65s] VO: "{feature 1} fixes {symptom 1}. {feature 2} fixes {symptom 2}…"

```
