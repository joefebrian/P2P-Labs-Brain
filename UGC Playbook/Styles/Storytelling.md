# Storytelling

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A personal narrative told in medias res (a divorce, a robbery, a turning point), with the product entering naturally at the turning point. It is the longest talking style and the most emotional.

## When to use it
Use it for high-consideration purchases, brand affinity, and creator personal-brand plays ('how I went from X to Y'). It fits cold audiences on Reels and YouTube Shorts, which tolerate longer videos. For AIOSCreator: 'how I quit my 9-5 with affiliate content'-type stories, told only if true.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **moment** | 0–5s | A mid-story line with emotional stakes |
| 2 | **context** | 5–20s | Who, what, why it mattered |
| 3 | **turning point** | 20–35s | The product enters naturally |
| 4 | **change** | 35–50s | Specific life details after |
| 5 | **reflection + soft cta** | last 8s | A lesson + offer |

## Hook formulas
- `The year after my {life event}, I stopped {doing X}.`
- `This is how we got {robbed/burned/scammed}.`
- `I went to {place} for {something I didn't do}. (absurd open)`
- `Everyone wants {aspiration}, but not me.`
- `My {partner} is freaking obsessed with this {product}.`
- `I used to spend hours {struggle}.`

## Script structure as observed
in-medias-res personal moment -> context/stakes (emotional) -> turning point where the product enters naturally -> how life changed (specific details) -> reflective line -> soft CTA. Longest talking style (median ~60s+, up to 131s); lower product density; brand arrives late.

## CTA patterns observed
- "And right now, there's actually a site-wide sale on, so click below and grab yours before it's gone" (Ekster 3)
- "Take a look at the Foldie and try it completely risk-free today." (Foldie 7)
- "Sign up today for free." (Janice Journal 1)
- "RYZE is 25% off right now and it comes with a 30 day money back guarantee" (RYZE 4)

## What the strongest examples do
- An in-medias-res line with real stakes (divorce, robbery).
- The product enters at the turning point, never at the start.
- Specific sensory details make it believable; a soft CTA preserves the tone.
