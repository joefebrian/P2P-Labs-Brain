# Street Interview

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
Handheld interviews with strangers: ask a relatable question, run a taste or try test, capture reactions in a montage, and have the interviewer read the offer.

## When to use it
Use it for mass-appeal products that are easy to try on the spot (food, drinks) and social-proof-at-scale messaging. For software, ask creators at events 'How long does a script take you?' and demo it on a phone.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **question** | 0–3s | 'Do you {habit}?' / 'Excuse me…' |
| 2 | **answers** | 3–12s | Relatable replies |
| 3 | **try** | 12–25s | The stranger tries it on camera |
| 4 | **reaction montage** | 25–40s | 3–5 quick genuine reactions |
| 5 | **offer** | last 5s | The interviewer reads the offer |

## Hook formulas
- `Do you normally {habit}?`
- `Hey, excuse me, {sir}? (+ controversial claim caption)`
- `Which one of you is the {brains} in the relationship?`
- `I'd love to get your opinion on this.`
- `Asking strangers if they {habit}.`
- `Today we're giving out {product} in {place}. Let's see what people think.`

## Script structure as observed
question to a stranger -> quick answers (relatable) -> taste/try on camera -> genuine reaction quote ("It's not like mushy, it goes down smooth and yeah, it's tasty." Oats 10) -> multiple people montage -> offer read by interviewer -> CTA. Median ~42s.

## CTA patterns observed
- "You get 25% off your first purchase and you get your first shaker bottle free." (Oats 10/2)
- "Check it out, go to everydaydose.com, click the link" (Everyday Dose 4)
- sweepstakes: "Shop at [site] to automatically enter to win." (Caraway 5)

## What the strongest examples do
- Relatable questions get relatable answers, so viewers see themselves in them.
- Multiple strangers' reactions add social proof at scale.
- The interviewer reads a concrete offer (25% off + free shaker).

