# Ultra Cinematic

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
Commercial-grade brand film: macro, slow-mo, colour-graded, evocative VO or none, a hero product moment, a proof beat, and a tagline end card.

## When to use it
Use it for brand awareness, premium positioning, launches, and top-of-funnel on Reels and YouTube. It is less suited to hard conversion. For AIOSCreator, use MotionControl to create cinematic UI and product shots.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **evocative open** | 0–3s | A sensory line or visual ('Do you hear that?') |
| 2 | **hero moment** | 3–15s | Product in macro or slow-mo |
| 3 | **proof** | 15–25s | Test / reviews / athlete |
| 4 | **tagline end card** | last 3–5s | Brand + CTA |

## Hook formulas
- `It says {claim}, but here at {brand}, we don't just say it, we test it.`
- `There's a reason {brand} has over {N} 5-star reviews.`
- `Do you hear that?`
- `{Essential truth about category}.`
- `The purpose of {engineering/design} is to {outcome}.`
- `This is the one {upgrade} I wish I'd made years ago.`

## Script structure as observed
short brand-film: evocative line / sensory open -> hero product moment (macro, slow-mo) -> 1-2 proof beats (test, reviews, athlete) -> tagline/end card. Median ~30s, short (IQR ~15-50s). Minimal text; cinematic end card.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Try it for only $10." (Harry's 14)
- "Try WHOOP today!" (crodtv 22)

## What the strongest examples do
- A sensory open (sound, macro) and a single evocative line.
- A proof beat inside the film ('we don't just say it, we test it', '50,000 5-star reviews').
- It ends on a tagline card and works for awareness rather than hard conversion.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** high-production commercial look: shallow depth of field, color-graded, slow-mo, macro product (earplug 3D render turntable; supplement dropper; eggs in a HexClad pan with the headline "WHY HEXCLAD COSTS OVER $1,000"; chef Sam Holland cooking; a 3D dental/tooth cavity animation "This portion of the video has been digitally created" - Hismile 25/36; a dark reveal with a red ribbon "THE PURPOSE OF ENGINEERING" - HexClad 34; architecture/lifestyle drone shots - Mott & Bow 19; athletes in gym - Gymshark; golden-hour lifestyle - Cymbiotika 37, AG1 30). Many are 6-17s music-only brand spots (37% low-speech).

**Spec:** 9:16. 24–30 fps cinematic grade, shallow DOF, macro and slow motion, sound design, minimal text. Median about 30 s; 37% have almost no speech.

**Categories seen:** cookware, electrolytes, wearables (WHOOP), supplements (AG1, Cymbiotika, IM8), razors, phone-blocker (Brick), oral care (Hismile), apparel. For AIOSCreator: MotionControl module - cinematic product/UI shots, brand-awareness spot.
