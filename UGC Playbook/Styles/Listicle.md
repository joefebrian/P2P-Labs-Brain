# Listicle

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A numbered list ('3 reasons…', '5 signs…', '3 lies…'), with a numbered on-screen card for each point. It promises structure and keeps viewers watching to the end.

## When to use it
Use it for products with several distinct benefits, for myth or mistake content, for comparisons, and for evergreen education. It is the easiest style to template.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **number hook** | 0–2s | '{N} reasons/signs/lies/ways…' as text and VO |
| 2 | **item 1** | 2–15s | The strongest or most surprising item |
| 3 | **item 2** | 15–28s | A benefit with a demo |
| 4 | **item 3** | 28–42s | Often the guarantee, offer or social proof |
| 5 | **cta** | last 3–5s | Risk-free / grab yours |

## Hook formulas
- `{N} reasons why {product} is actually worth the price.`
- `{N} reasons {group} hate {product}. (reverse)`
- `{N} lies the {industry} has been selling you about {topic}.`
- `{N} signs your {thing} is {failing}.`
- `{N} ways this {product} saves you money every time you {activity}.`
- `Do not buy {product} until you hear this.`
- `{N} things that {bad outcome}.`

## Script structure as observed
number promise in 0-2s (text + VO) -> reason 1 (strongest/most surprising) -> reason 2 -> reason 3 (often the offer/guarantee as the final "reason") -> CTA. Each reason ~10-15s with its own numbered on-screen card. Median ~50s. Variants: lies/myths, signs/symptoms, mistakes, situations, ways it saves money.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Take a look at the Foldie and try it risk-free for 60 days." (The Foldie 26/27)
- "So run to Shapermint to grab yours before it sells out again." (Shapermint 17)
- "But it's always selling out like crazy so run to their website to get yours before it's too late." (DRMTLGY 1)
- "True Classic cannot guarantee you a wife." (True Classic 20 - humorous close)

## What the strongest examples do
- The number in frame 0 creates an open loop that viewers stay for.
- Reversal titles ('3 reasons single guys hate True Classic') add curiosity.
- The guarantee or offer is framed as the final 'reason', so the CTA doesn't feel like a pitch.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** numbered headline card top/middle ("5 Reasons you need THESE shirts", "3 Reasons Single Guys Hate True Classic", "3 REASONS WHY THE 12 PIECE SET IS WORTH THE PRICE", "5 situations where this 40L bag becomes a lifesaver"), creator talking head or product demo; each reason gets a numbered caption card; Barbie doll meme opener (DRMTLGY 1); try-on in store. Images: static listicle ads.

**Spec:** 9:16. A persistent header card with the number plus a big numbered caption per item. Talking head or demo. Median about 50 s; about 12–15 s per item. The number must appear in frame 0.

**Categories seen:** shapewear, apparel, travel bags, skincare, supplements, cookware, jewellery (The Ridge), drinks (BREZ).

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–2s] TEXT+VO: "{N} {reasons/signs/lies} {promise}."
[2–15s] TEXT "1." VO: "{item 1}" SHOT: {demo}
[15–28s] TEXT "2." VO: "{item 2}" SHOT: {demo}
[28–40s] TEXT "3." VO: "{item 3 — offer/guarantee}"
[40–45s] VO: "{CTA}." TEXT: "#ad"
```
