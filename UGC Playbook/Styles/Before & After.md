# Before & After

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
The transformation is the proof: split-screen, side-by-side, or a before → reveal. Often music-only, with text overlays.

## When to use it
Use it when the result is visual and fast (skin, fit, a messy desk turned tidy, a raw clip turned an edited clip, an empty calendar turned a full one). It suits retargeting and product-aware audiences. For software, compare the output before and after the tool.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **after/split first** | 0–2s | Show both states at once, or the after first |
| 2 | **before pain** | 2–8s | What life was like; the specific flaw |
| 3 | **bridge** | 8–15s | The product or switch that caused it |
| 4 | **process** | 15–30s | Real-time demo of the change |
| 5 | **proof/timeframe** | +5s | 'in one week', '30 days', numbers |
| 6 | **cta** | last 3s | Where to get it |

## Hook formulas
- `{Time} ago versus now.`
- `The {thing} you're {using} versus the one that actually {result}.`
- `Wrong {X} on the left. Right {X} on the right.`
- `This is what happened when I {did habit} every day for {N} days.`
- `The difference between this {raw X} and this {finished X} is just a few clicks in {tool}.`
- `Why {N} {people} switched to this {product}.`

## Script structure as observed
show the AFTER (or split) in frame 1 -> name the before pain -> the switch/product -> demo of the transformation in real time -> proof/time frame ("in one week", "30 days") -> CTA. Transformation must be visible in 0-3s.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Grab yours at jonesroadbeauty.com." (Jones Road 18/55)
- "It's sold in the TikTok shop. Grab it in the TikTok shop linked below and let me know what you think." (DRMTLGY 37)
- "I will never, ever, ever buy bras anywhere else." (Shapermint 5)

## What the strongest examples do
- The transformation is visible in the first 2 s (split-screen), so there's no waiting.
- Matched framing makes the difference undeniable, and text labels carry it with sound off.
- A time frame ('in one week', '30 days') makes the result believable.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** split-screen or side-by-side (half face with/without concealer - DRMTLGY 1; two bras side by side WRONG vs RIGHT - Shapermint 5; baggy vs fitted tee - True Classic 3/41; flat butt jeans on 60-y-o man - Mott & Bow 26), mirror/selfie framing, pregnant-belly to shapewear dress reveal (HeyShape 29), stock "before/after" labels in white caption pills. Text pills say BEFORE and AFTER. 30% of the videos are music-only (<15 words), so the text must carry the message. ## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–2s] SPLIT: {before} | {after} TEXT: "{before} vs {after}"
[2–8s] VO: "This was me {before state + specific pain}."
[8–15s] VO: "Then I switched to {product}." SHOT: product
[15–30s] VO/SHOT: {process in real time}
[30–35s] VO: "{result + timeframe}." TEXT: "{metric}"
[35–38s] CTA: "{CTA}" TEXT: "#ad"
```
