# Problem / Solution

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
The core UGC frame. Call out a specific pain, agitate it with details, then 'until I found this', demo, proof and offer. This is the second-largest category (102 files) and the most versatile.

## When to use it
Use it as the default for problem-aware audiences and any product that removes a clear pain, including SaaS and creator tools (Canva, Motion and Withings all appear here). If unsure, start with this style.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **callout + pain** | 0–3s | 'If you {pain}, listen to this' / stat / command |
| 2 | **agitate** | 3–10s | Specific, relatable consequences |
| 3 | **reveal** | 8–12s | 'Until I found…' + product |
| 4 | **demo/how** | 12–30s | How it fixes the pain |
| 5 | **proof** | 30–38s | Result, numbers, reviews |
| 6 | **offer + cta** | last 5s | Trial / % off / guarantee |

## Hook formulas
- `If you've got {trait} and you hate {task}, listen to this.`
- `If you {embarrassing symptom}, this one's for you.`
- `Did you know {stat about the problem}?`
- `Stop using {common item} in your {place}.`
- `Ever notice how {small annoyance} ruins {moment}?`
- `I'm officially done {struggling with X}.`
- `{Content/work} is all starting to look like {the same slop}.`
- `Here are two {pages/examples}. One of them is {failing}.`
- `We often forget how much time we lose {doing X}.`

## Script structure as observed
callout/pain 0-3s -> agitate w/ specifics (3-10s) -> "until I found / then I found this" (reveal ~8-12s) -> how it works/demo -> result/proof -> offer + CTA. Median ~43s. Motion 63 is the tightest example: 15s, 62 words: pain + quantified loss (8 hours a week) + tool + result (month planned in 5 min) + free 7 days.

## CTA patterns observed
- "It's free for 7 days, so give it a shot." (Motion 63)
- "Try it for 30 days at withings.com." (Savvy Finds 36)
- "comment Kanva and I'll send you exactly how I set up mine" (Artzen 61 - comment-keyword CTA)
- "Try the DeepSleep Mask risk-free for 60 days with a special 25% off sale." (Dore & Rose 3)
- "It's time to ditch the shedding microplastics and upgrade to Caraway today." (Kelsey G 76)

## What the strongest examples do
- A hyper-specific callout ('thick thighs + hate buying jeans online') self-selects the buyer.
- Quantified pain ('8 hours a week, just gone') beats vague pain.
- The fastest winners are tiny: 15 s, 62 words, pain → tool → result → free trial (Motion 63).

**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** creator talking head holding the "problem" item (old earplugs, foam; wallet - Ekster 38; plastic cutting board), headline caption top ("I wish I knew this when I was shopping for this wedding season", "Not proud of this, but I'll say it anyway", "If you're wearing cami's"), extreme close-up of the problem (nose pores - DRMTLGY 66), stressed person at a table with pills/bills (Savvy Finds 47 - healthcare service), telehealth app UI (hims 21), six-bride lineup (Shapermint 1). Then a cut to the solution demo.

**Spec:** 9:16. Talking head holding the problem item, then a demo cut; a headline caption top-third; a cut every 3 s. Median about 43 s. The tightest winner is 15 s (Motion 63).

**Categories seen:** widest mix: apparel/shapewear, supplements, skincare, travel, healthcare services (Solace), SaaS (Canva, Motion), smart scales (Withings), cookware.

```text
[10–13s] VO: "Until I found {product}."
[13–28s] VO: "{how it works: 2–3 steps/features}." SHOT: {demo}
[28–35s] VO: "{result + proof}."
[35–40s] VO: "{offer}. {CTA}." TEXT: "#ad"
```
