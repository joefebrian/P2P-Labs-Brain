# Myth Bust

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
State a common myth (with MYTH on screen), explain why people believe it, reveal the truth, then position the product as the correct approach.

## When to use it
Use it for categories full of misconceptions (supplements, AI tools: 'AI content gets shadowbanned', 'you need to show your face'). It is educational top-of-funnel content that builds authority. The sample is small (5 videos), so blend it with the Listicle and Green Screen patterns.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **myth** | 0–3s | 'Myth:' plus the belief as text |
| 2 | **why believed** | 3–12s | Empathise |
| 3 | **truth** | 12–30s | Mechanism or evidence |
| 4 | **product** | 30–45s | The correct approach |
| 5 | **cta** | last 5s | Risk-free trial |

## Hook formulas
- `Myth: {common belief}.`
- `Most people have the wrong idea when it comes to {topic}.`
- `Stop {wasting X}.`
- `If you're {noticing symptom}, {hidden cause} may be why.`
- `If you {do X}, you need to be careful with {Y}, because here's what could happen.`

### Real hooks observed (verbatim transcript/on-screen text, attributed by file `style-Ad-N-Brand`)

## Script structure as observed
state myth (text MYTH) -> why people believe it -> the truth/mechanism -> product as the correct approach -> risk-free CTA. Long for RYZE (90-108s), short for David Protein (25s).

## CTA patterns observed
- "Try Seed's DS-01 risk-free for 30 days." (Seed 3)
- "You can try it completely risk free for 30 days so you have nothing to lose. Tap the link below" (RYZE 1)
- "Try out RYZE risk free for 30 days." (RYZE 5)

## What the strongest examples do
- The MYTH card is a pattern interrupt, and people love being told they were wrong.
- The empathy beat ('I get why people think that') keeps it from feeling preachy.
- Risk-free trial CTAs pair with the 'you have nothing to lose' logic.
