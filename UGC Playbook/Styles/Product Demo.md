# Product Demo

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
The product is in hand from frame 0: what it is, live demo of the key feature, a sensory or result close-up, then a verdict.

## When to use it
Use it when the product's mechanism is impressive on camera, for solution- and product-aware audiences, and for TikTok Shop-style listings. For software, use a 'watch me do X in 30 seconds' timed demo (Canva 18 is a launch-poster challenge).

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **product-in-frame hook** | 0–3s | Product visible plus a claim or challenge |
| 2 | **what it is** | 3–6s | One line (brand mention median about 4 s) |
| 3 | **demo** | 6–25s | Feature by feature, close-ups |
| 4 | **result** | 25–32s | Outcome shot |
| 5 | **verdict + cta** | last 5s | 'If you only buy one thing…' + link |

## Hook formulas
- `Let's {prepare/make X} in {30 seconds}.`
- `I've never recommended a product this quickly before.`
- `This is the most underrated {category}.`
- `Let's see if the internet is lying about this product.`
- `I thought these {product} ads were just hype. Then I actually tried them.`
- `This is {N grams / spec}.`
- `Don't buy this unless you're looking for {problems}. (ironic)`
- `{Brand} sent me a script for this video and honestly I didn't want to use it.`

## Script structure as observed
product visible 0s -> what it is in one line -> live demo of the key feature(s) step-by-step -> sensory/result close-up -> verdict ("If you're only gonna buy one thing, this is the one thing." DRMTLGY 21) -> CTA. Median ~36s. Brand/product mentioned very early.

## CTA patterns observed
- "If you're thinking about upgrading your kitchen, grab HexClad today and save when you buy the set." (HexClad 27)
- "Grab the discount link in my bio and don't frown." (Kind Patches 15)
- "Try Canva AI today." (Canva 18)
- "And I can get 10% off if you subscribe and new customers can get 30 pounds in free gifts. So check them out." (Huel 8)

## What the strongest examples do
- The product is in hand at the lens in frame 0.
- Timed challenges ('in 30 seconds', 'before…') add stakes to a demo.
- A verdict line ('If you're only gonna buy one thing, this is the one thing') closes.

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–3s] SHOT: product at lens VO: "{demo hook}."
[3–6s] VO: "This is {product}: {one-liner}."
[6–14s] VO: "{feature 1 demo}." SHOT: {close-up}
[14–22s] VO: "{feature 2 demo}."
[22–30s] VO: "{result}." SHOT: {outcome}
[30–35s] VO: "{verdict}. {offer + CTA}."
```
