# Rage Bait

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A provocative or negative opening ('Don't buy X', 'This is a scam', 'This feels illegal') that triggers comments. It then twists into a positive reason, or reveals that the competitors are the scam.

## When to use it
Use it for mature, saturated categories where attention is expensive, and when you can pay off the twist honestly. Avoid false claims about competitors and never mislead; the twist must be true.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **bait** | 0–3s | Negative or controversial claim, big text |
| 2 | **twist** | 3–8s | '…unless / until…' flips it |
| 3 | **reason** | 8–30s | The real benefits |
| 4 | **proof** | 30–40s | Numbers, comparison |
| 5 | **urgent offer** | last 5–8s | Deadline / BOGO / selling out |

## Hook formulas
- `Don't buy {product}. (…until you understand {benefit})`
- `This is a scam, this is a scam, and this is a scam too. (competitors → yours)`
- `This honestly feels illegal.`
- `Genuinely, why are people still {using outdated method}?`
- `Stop throwing your cash away.`
- `I'm going to have to return this product. (twist)`
- `Free {product} for everyone. (BOGO)`

## Script structure as observed
negative/controversial claim 0s (triggers comments) -> twist: "...unless / until you know" -> real reason it's good (or competitors are the scam) -> proof -> offer w/ urgency -> CTA. Median ~52s. Keep the bait honest (the twist must pay off, no false claims about competitors).

## CTA patterns observed
- "But on their official TikTok shop you can get all three for less than the price of one." (Goli 15)
- "This incredible offer is only available until the end of the day so grab yours before it's gone" (The Foldie 17)
- "30 nights risk-free" (Savvy Finds 1)
- "Oh, and a huge sale. Free shipping and free gifts in order. I'll put a link below." (RYZE 2)

## What the strongest examples do
- The negative claim drives comments and dwell; the twist arrives within about 8 s.
- Honest payoff: competitors-as-scam or 'don't buy… until you know'.
- Pairs with hard urgency (end-of-day deal, BOGO).
