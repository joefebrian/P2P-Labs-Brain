# B-Roll + Voiceover

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
Faceless or semi-faceless product b-roll (textures, use, lifestyle) under a creator-style voiceover, with a big headline caption. It is the largest category in the dataset (160 files).

## When to use it
Use it for fast, cheap volume testing: swap hooks over the same b-roll. It suits physical products with satisfying visuals, and software when you have screen b-roll. It is the natural format for the Faceless VO module.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **hook** | 0–3s | Headline VO + the same text; the first frame is in motion (pour, squeeze, unfold) |
| 2 | **product in** | 3–8s | Name or show the product |
| 3 | **3 benefits** | 8–25s | One benefit per b-roll shot (a cut every 2–3 s) |
| 4 | **proof** | 25–30s | 'thousands of 5-star reviews', 'sold out 3×', ratings |
| 5 | **offer + cta** | last 5s | % off / code / risk-free + 'tap below' |

## Hook formulas
- `{Price}? Here's why it's actually cheap.`
- `This is the {product} for people who don't {use product category}.`
- `If you hate {problem}, you need to watch this.`
- `Why do {experts} hate this {product}?`
- `I thought this was one of those {Facebook products} that never lives up to the hype.`
- `Watch this {small thing} turn into {impressive result}.`
- `Let me save you {N years} of {mistakes} in {30 seconds}.`
- `Yes, we're {pricey}, and that's because {reason}.`

## Script structure as observed
short (median ~35s), fast VO ~100-150 words: hook headline -> product intro in 3-8s -> 3 benefits over matching b-roll -> proof (reviews "thousands of 5 star reviews", "sold out 3x") -> offer + scarcity -> CTA. Pure VO means scripts can be swapped over the same b-roll (efficient for testing hooks).

## What the strongest examples do
- The hook is said and shown as a big headline over a motion shot in frame 0.
- One benefit per shot with a cut every 2–3 s. The best ones use surprising contrasts ('$1,000 for pots and pans?' → 'why it's actually cheap').
- Proof uses concrete social numbers ('sold out 3×', 'thousands of 5-star reviews'), then scarcity.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** see sheet. Hand-held product b-roll, lifestyle clips, texture/close-ups, packaging, try-on; the VO is a creator voice (sometimes a founder/employee). Big caption headline repeating the hook at 0-3s (TXT matches the VO in most cases). Many are "staff/POV of company": "Let's pack an order for Kelly from Nebraska." (Oats Overnight 99), "I just started working at Oats Overnight." (148). extreme close-ups of product texture (protein bars, green smoothie pour, cream in a jar), a face half-with/half-without makeup, an egg with a drawn face for "hot girl hack" (DRMTLGY 155), a concert crowd (Loop 4), a rack of tees (Savvy Finds 54), a gym (Arrae 68), a creator in bra try-on. Captions are small white TikTok-native text boxes mid-frame, or a big headline. Opening frames are often motion (pour, squeeze) = pattern interrupt.

**Spec:** 9:16. Close-up macro b-roll plus in-use shots plus the result; a cut every 2–3 s. A bold headline repeats the hook for 0–3 s, then small TikTok-native captions. VO about 170–190 wpm, conversational. Median 35 s (IQR 28–50 s).

**Categories seen:** food/protein (Oats Overnight dominant), shapewear/bras (Shapermint, SheCurve), skincare (DRMTLGY, Dore & Rose, Gleamin, Jones Road), travel (The Foldie, Loop), men's health (Mars Men), phone accessories (BURGA), cookware (HexClad), fitness apps (BetterMe).

