# Motion Graphics

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
Pure brand motion design: the product pack or UI on bold gradients with kinetic typography, one claim per card. It is short, and there is usually no VO.

## When to use it
Use it for retargeting, offer announcements ($ off, new flavour or feature launch), social-proof bumpers ('Just ask TikTok'), and 6–15 s placements. It is cheap to produce at scale with MotionControl and Canva-style templates.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **attention card** | 0–2s | NEW / OFFER / social proof headline |
| 2 | **benefit cards** | 2–7s | 1 benefit per card, 3–7 words, about 1.5–2 s each |
| 3 | **hero** | 7–9s | Product or UI hero with motion |
| 4 | **end card** | last 1–2s | 'Shop Now' / 'Try free' + offer |

## Hook formulas
- `NEW! {variant/feature}`
- `GET {$X} OFF — the {duo/tool} that does it all`
- `Our #1 {selling product} for a reason!`
- `{Platform} loves {product}`
- `For {benefit} without {downside}`
- `Want to {beat problem}? Start at the source.`
- `SAY HELLO TO {new benefit}`

## Script structure as observed
1 claim per card, 3-5 cards: attention card (new/offer/social proof) -> benefit cards -> product hero -> "Shop Now". Text 3-7 words per card, each card ~1.5-2s.

## CTA patterns observed
- Shop Now" end card; "Start DS01 today!" (Seed 5)

## What the strongest examples do
- One idea per card, 3–7 words, strong brand colour, product always in frame.
- Opens on NEW, an offer, or social proof ('#1 selling', 'Just ask TikTok').
- Short (median ~9 s) so it completes, which is ideal for retargeting.

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–2s] CARD: "{ATTENTION: NEW / $ OFF / #1}" + {hero motion}
[2–4s] CARD: "{benefit 1}"
[4–6s] CARD: "{benefit 2}"
[6–8s] CARD: "{benefit 3 / social proof}"
[8–10s] END: "{Shop Now / Try free} · {offer}"
```
