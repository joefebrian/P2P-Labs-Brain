# Founder-led

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
The founder or CEO speaks to camera: the origin story, why they built it, and what makes it different. It borrows authority and authenticity from the founder.

## When to use it
Use it for brand trust, new launches, premium pricing, and differentiation against copycats. For AIOSCreator, P2P Labs' founder explains why local-first matters. It is not for affiliates, unless they interview the founder.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **identity/hook** | 0–3s | 'I'm {name}, founder of {brand}' or an origin pain line |
| 2 | **origin problem** | 3–15s | The personal frustration |
| 3 | **build** | 15–25s | 'So I built…' plus the key insight |
| 4 | **difference** | 25–35s | Mechanism, materials, approach |
| 5 | **proof** | 35–42s | A customer quote or number |
| 6 | **guarantee + cta** | last 5s | Risk reversal plus link |

## Hook formulas
- `I'm {name}, the CEO and founder of {brand}.`
- `I went to {N} {experts} and all they could suggest was {bad option}.`
- `{A problem} that took me nearly {N} years to solve.`
- `This is the last {product} you will ever buy.`
- `This is a {product} for people who don't like {category}.`
- `My team just built an internal tool for {job}… and we made it free.`

## Script structure as observed
identity/credibility -> personal problem/origin -> "so I built X" -> what makes it different (mechanism, materials) -> customer proof ("When we get a review... it's the last bag they'll ever use") -> guarantee -> CTA. Median ~47s, conversational wpm.

## CTA patterns observed
- "We offer this 60-day money-back guarantee so that you can try it too completely risk-free." (FLO 1)
- "Click the link now and start traveling with confidence again." (The Foldie 22)

## What the strongest examples do
- Specific origin pain ('I went to ten dermatologists') beats generic mission talk.
- Differentiation is stated against a named alternative.
- A guarantee from the founder's mouth doubles as a credibility signal.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** founder talking head to camera in office/warehouse/showroom, lower-third name/title ("I'm Danny Weiner, the CEO and founder of HexClad"), big bold white word captions, "MY FOUNDER STORY" + Forbes press card (sammicohentalks 14), comment sticker used by founders too (Jones Road 16 "ALSO A HATER"), Bobbi Brown (Jones Road) holding product close to lens, packing warehouse (Foldie 22), shirtless founder w/ headphones podcast-style (Everyday Dose 20).

**Spec:** 9:16. Founder medium close-up in the office, warehouse or studio; a lower-third name and title; bold captions. Occasional b-roll of the product or team. Median 47 s, conversational pace.

**Categories seen:** beauty (Jones Road/Bobbi Brown), shapewear (Pinsy), cookware (HexClad), travel bags, supplements, business/info products (Hormozi). Strong fit for AIOSCreator's own founder (P2P Labs).

## Fill-in script template

```text
[3–15s] VO: "{Origin story: the pain}."

[25–35s] VO: "Unlike {alternatives}, it {difference}."

[42–47s] VO: "{Guarantee/offer}. {CTA}."
```
