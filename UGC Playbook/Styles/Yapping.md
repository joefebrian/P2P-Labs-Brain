# Yapping

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
Long, casual, chatty talking-head monologues ('get ready with me', car or couch rants), where the product is woven into a stream-of-consciousness conversation. It is high-intimacy, creator-native content.

## When to use it
Use it for creator-led affiliate content with a loyal audience, lifestyle products used daily, and Spark Ads from real creators. It is the most 'organic'-feeling style.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **chatty hook** | 0–3s | A casual confession, opinion or story |
| 2 | **context** | 3–20s | Life tangent |
| 3 | **product woven in** | 20–40s | A natural mention and use |
| 4 | **honest take** | 40–55s | Pros plus a mild con |
| 5 | **soft cta** | last 5s | 'Linked it for you' |

## Hook formulas
- `Okay, I need to talk about {thing} because {reason}.`
- `Get ready with me while I tell you about {story}.`
- `I've been {doing X} for {time} and nobody talks about {Y}.`
- `Unpopular opinion: {opinion}.`

## Script structure as observed
confession/opinion open -> personal context & tangent -> product used on camera while talking -> honest details (shade match, taste, routine) -> code/link. Long, conversational; high trust. CTAs are soft and personal.

- "Go get one before they sell out again, and thank me later." (Suhaine 32)

## What the strongest examples do
- Feels like FaceTime with a friend: intimacy converts loyal audiences.
- The product is woven into a story rather than pitched.
- An honest mild con increases trust.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** single creator close-up, selfie framing (bathroom/vanity/bedroom/car/kitchen), applying makeup or holding a supplement at the lens while talking, jump cuts, small TikTok-native captions, occasional green-screen photo collage (Leah 22 Italy trip), discount-code overlay ("use my code 25MANDYB" - Mandy Brooke 4, "Use Tawny for a discount + a welcome gift" - Tawny Janae 7), "Learn the goods" TikTok Shop anchor. Median ~45s, the most words per video.

**Spec:** 9:16. A single-take talking head (car, couch, vanity); jump cuts; captions; high wpm; long (see the stats). Light scripting: bullet points, not lines.

**Categories seen:** supplements (creatine gummies Arrae, Cymbiotika, IM8, Seed), beauty (Jones Road, DRMTLGY, Gleamin), bras, phone-blocker (Brick), fragrance, food. Creator-affiliate native.
