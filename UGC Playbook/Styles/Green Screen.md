# Green Screen

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
The creator sits in the lower half of the frame in front of a background of evidence: a study, a review page, a product page, a news headline, bloodwork, an offer banner. The creator explains or reacts.

## When to use it
Use it for education, news-jacking, comparisons, 'the science', review-count proof, and showing landing pages or dashboards. It is great for software (a screenshot of the dashboard behind you).

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **evidence hook** | 0–3s | The background shows the stat or headline; the VO states it |
| 2 | **explain** | 3–15s | What it means for the viewer |
| 3 | **product as answer** | 15–25s | Swap the background to the product or landing page |
| 4 | **proof** | 25–35s | Reviews, numbers, screenshots |
| 5 | **cta** | last 5s | Offer banner in the background |

## Hook formulas
- `A recent study found {stat}.`
- `Take this from a {credentialed person}.`
- `The most proven {ingredient/method} for {result} is {X}.`
- `This is your sign to stop {old way} and switch to this.`
- `The biggest issue with {popular alternative} is {flaw}.`
- `We always get asked why {product} is better than {alternative}.`
- `We have over {N} {variants} and today we're ranking them.`

## Script structure as observed
background evidence visible at 0s (study, screenshot, comparison) -> creator explains/reacts -> product as the answer -> proof screenshot (reviews, bloodwork) -> offer -> CTA. Median ~40s. Great for "explaining" + comparisons + news-jacking.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Order Cupid's Hypnosis 3.0 at the link below. It comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee." (Try Cupids 30)
- "I wish I saw this sooner, but Mars Men is now 50% off... So if you don't wanna pay more later, I'm gonna leave the link down below." (cole_schwartzz 33)
- "Alright, so go click on the link below and feel the difference for yourself." (Mars Men 21)
- "So if you want to get it in time for Mother's Day the best time to order is going to be right now." (PupRing 27)

## What the strongest examples do
- Evidence (study, review count, headline) is visible at 0 s, so the claim feels sourced.
- The creator points at and reacts to the background, making it a conversation rather than a pitch.
- The background changes every beat: evidence → product → proof → offer banner.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** creator in bottom 40-50% of frame, background = screenshot (product page with review count "64,281 reviews", Q&A "Why not instant oatmeal or eggs?"), study/article/news headline (Caraway microplastics study), product close-up photo, before/after photo, lab bloodwork report (Mars Men 34), offer banner ("CLAIM YOUR OFFER 50% OFF $70 FREE GIFTS" cole_schwartzz 33), Kaboom-style meme. Creator points at background. Captions white boxes mid-frame.

**Spec:** 9:16. Creator cut out over the bottom 40–50%; the background image fills the top; point at the evidence. Change the background every 5–8 s. Median about 40 s.

**Categories seen:** supplements/men's health, skincare, cookware (Caraway non-toxic), food, earplugs, pet gifts, fragrance.

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–3s] BG: {study/headline/screenshot} VO: "{stat/claim hook}."
[3–15s] VO: "{What that means + why it matters to you}."
[15–25s] BG: {product page} VO: "That's why I use {product}: {mechanism}."
[25–35s] BG: {reviews/results} VO: "{proof}."
[35–40s] BG: {offer banner} VO: "{offer + CTA}."
```
