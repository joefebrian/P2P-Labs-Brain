# Testimonial

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A real customer or expert shares their experience: skepticism, a specific timeline, concrete outcomes, a recommendation. It includes supercut formats and static review-card images (34 images in this folder).

## When to use it
Use it for conversion and retargeting, for skeptical audiences, and for B2B or info products (the Hormozi supercut). It needs real customers and real quotes; never fabricate testimonials.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **identity/hook** | 0–3s | Name/role or a surprising outcome line |
| 2 | **skepticism** | 3–10s | 'I wasn't expecting…' |
| 3 | **timeline** | 10–30s | 'Around week 2… by day 30…' |
| 4 | **outcome** | 30–40s | Concrete numbers |
| 5 | **recommend + offer** | last 5–8s | Free kit / link |

## Hook formulas
- `I wasn't expecting to notice {difference} quickly, but here's what happened after {N} days of {product}.`
- `I had no idea my {old item} was {causing problem}.`
- `So there's something I've been {using}, and no, it's not {obvious thing}.`
- `I couldn't really tell if {category} was working until I started {product}.`
- `Hi, my name is {name} and I'm {role} at {company}.`
- `(Supercut) '{outcome soundbite}' × 5 customers`

## Script structure as observed
identity (name/role or relatable "before" state) -> skepticism ("I wasn't expecting...", "I was pretty skeptical at first") -> the specific experience with timeline ("Around the two-week mark... By the end of the month") -> concrete outcome numbers -> recommendation -> offer (free welcome kit, link). Multi-customer supercut for B2B (Hormozi): quick outcome soundbites stitched together.

## CTA patterns observed
- "If you want to try it, you get a free welcome kit with your first subscription order." (Kenzie Blair 21)
- "There's a link below, so make sure you hit it" (WHOOP 4)
- "Visit seed.com to start today." (Seed 37)
- "Click through now to get one before they run out." (Dore & Rose 10)

## What the strongest examples do
- Skepticism first ('I wasn't expecting…'), then a specific timeline ('around the two-week mark…').
- Numbers in soundbites ('doubled my open rate', 'raised price 25–50%').
- Name and role lower thirds and review screenshots add verifiability.
