# Voiceless

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
No speech at all: on-screen text plus music or trending audio carries the whole message over product or lifestyle footage. It is sound-off proof.

## When to use it
Use it for fast-scrolling placements, international audiences, visual products, and when no talent or VO is available. It suits Faceless and MotionControl outputs, and repurposing b-roll.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **text hook** | 0–2s | A bold 3–8 word claim or question over motion |
| 2 | **problem/context text** | 2–6s | Short caption |
| 3 | **product in use** | 6–15s | Visual demo with 1-line captions |
| 4 | **proof text** | 15–20s | Rating / result |
| 5 | **cta card** | last 2s | Shop / link |

## Hook formulas
- `{Result} in {time}.`
- `{N} years of {problem} ended in {N} days.`
- `The {product} that sold out {N}×.`
- `Why settle for {one}? (bundle)`
- `Me at {age}: {symptoms list} → But in just {N} days…`

## Script structure as observed
text hook over motion (0-2s) -> 2-4 caption beats over product use -> result/benefit text -> end (often no explicit CTA; relies on the TikTok Shop/product anchor). Median ~20s. 100% rely on OCR; our analysis used on-screen text + keyframes.

## CTA patterns observed
- mostly implicit (product link sticker)
- on-screen "link in bio"/"shop" cards when present.

## What the strongest examples do
- The text hook is readable in under 1.5 s and sits in the safe zone.
- Each card carries one idea; the visuals do the demo.
- Works globally and sound-off; cheap to version.
