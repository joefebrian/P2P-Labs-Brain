# POV

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A 'POV: …' caption drops the viewer into a relatable moment. It is often music-only and meme-like, with the product appearing as the treat or the fix. Long variants add a skit.

## When to use it
Use it for relatable-pain awareness, trend participation, and short 10–20 s loops. It suits products tied to a specific moment (deadline panic, packing, a skin day). It is easy to produce faceless.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **POV caption** | 0s | 'POV: {relatable situation}' |
| 2 | **moment** | 1–8s | Act out or show the feeling |
| 3 | **product** | 8–14s | The product appears as the fix |
| 4 | **payoff** | last 2–4s | Result or punchline (+ CTA for longer versions) |

## Hook formulas
- `POV: you {bought the viral X} and {result}.`
- `POV: you stopped {doing X} because {pain}.`
- `POV: {coworkers/friends} {situation}.`
- `POV: we're besties and I'm sharing {secret product}.`
- `POV: your {flight/deadline} is tomorrow and you still haven't {task}.`

## Script structure as observed
POV caption establishes a relatable situation (0s) -> the viewer "lives" the moment (reaction/visual gag) -> product appears as the fix/treat -> 1-line benefit -> end. Low VO; song/trend audio. Long variants add a skit.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Go to Mott & Bow and get yours today." (Mott & Bow 9)
- "It's so good it literally keeps selling out. So you'll probably want to grab your size" (Shapermint 6)
- "You can visit Canva Print Shop today and enjoy free delivery." (Canva 16)

## What the strongest examples do
- A relatable second-person situation in frame 0; the viewer 'is' the character.
- Very short loops (10–20 s) with trending audio are built for rewatches.
- The product is the punchline, not a pitch.

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–2s] TEXT: "POV: {situation}" SHOT: {creator reacting / scene}
[2–8s] SHOT: {the feeling, exaggerated}
[8–13s] SHOT: {product appears / used} TEXT: "{product} said: {fix}"
[13–16s] SHOT: {payoff} TEXT: "{CTA/short benefit}"
```
