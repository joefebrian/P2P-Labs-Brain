# Live Reaction

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
An unscripted-feeling first reaction from another person (partner, parent, friend) or a first taste or try. The reaction is the proof.

## When to use it
Use it for products with a 'wow' moment (taste, fit, a visible result, an impressive output). For software, show the output to a skeptical friend. It works for awareness and virality.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **setup** | 0–3s | Who is reacting to what ('Showing my husband…') |
| 2 | **reveal/try** | 3–15s | First try, on camera |
| 3 | **reaction** | 15–35s | Genuine surprise; skepticism flips to approval |
| 4 | **where'd you get it** | 35–45s | The second person asks, which opens the offer |
| 5 | **soft cta** | last 5s | Offer mention; 'should I get more?' |

## Hook formulas
- `{Person}, what the heck are these?`
- `I just got {product}, so I'm gonna show my {partner} and see their reaction.`
- `My {buddy} never {does X} and always complains about {pain}.`
- `I am so tired of {problem}… (fake-out) …`
- `Real {people} try {product} for the first time.`

## Script structure as observed
setup (who + what is being tested) -> first try / reveal -> genuine reaction (surprise, laughter, skepticism turning into approval) -> second person asks "where'd you get it" -> offer mention -> soft CTA. Long (median ~56s), low scripted density. The authenticity of the reaction is the proof.

## CTA patterns observed
- "They're having a BOGO sale right now, should I get some more?" (HeyShape 3)
- "And now they're having a promotion you can buy one free under $80 right now" (The Foldie 7)

## What the strongest examples do
- Real skepticism turning into delight is the proof, and it can't be faked in a script.
- The second person asks 'where'd you get it?', which lets the offer arrive naturally.
- A caption header names the situation so it lands with sound off.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** two people in frame (mom + daughter, husband + wife, buddies in car), unscripted feel, handheld, caption headline top ("My husband found my shapewear!", "My husband's reaction to me wearing shapewear"), first-taste reactions ("Real Moms taste Oats Overnight for the first time" Oats 6), a reaction over TV/video (Foldie 7 "These are amazing"), under-eye patch on an older woman with a skeptical face (DRMTLGY 8/10).

**Spec:** 9:16. Handheld, two people in frame, natural room audio plus captions; a caption header names the situation. Median about 56 s; light scripting (write bullet prompts, not lines).

**Categories seen:** shapewear, skincare/eye patches, food (first taste), weight loss supplements, travel gadgets, mushroom coffee.

## Fill-in script template
```text

[3–15s] {Person tries/sees product}
[15–35s] {Reaction beats: skeptical → surprised → approving}
[35–45s] PERSON 2: "Wait, where did you get this?" / "How much?"

```
