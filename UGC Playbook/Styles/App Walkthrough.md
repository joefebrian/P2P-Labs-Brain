# App Walkthrough

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A creator shows the app or software step by step: screen recording plus face or hands, with fast narration of 'here's how'. This is the most direct style for SaaS and AI tools.

## When to use it
Use it for any software (AIOSCreator itself), when the value is visible on screen and the audience is solution-aware, and for app installs, free trials and affiliate SaaS links. Pair it with Before & After for results.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **hook** | 0–3s | Relatable pain or desired outcome + 'here's how' |
| 2 | **setup** | 3–6s | Name the tool (median brand mention 6.5 s, about 26% of runtime) |
| 3 | **demo steps** | 6–25s | 2–4 steps: tap → choose → generate, each with a screen close-up |
| 4 | **result** | 25–32s | Show the finished output or the time saved |
| 5 | **cta** | last 3–5s | Free trial or link |

## Hook formulas
- `Here's my setup in {tool} that handles all my {busy work}.`
- `If your {thing} also looks like this, here's how you're gonna {outcome}.`
- `How to {earn/do X} as a {smaller creator/beginner}.`
- `What experienced {creators} won't tell you: {insight}.`
- `Stop {waiting for X}. There's a much easier way.`
- `This is how you do a whole {task} with just your phone and {tool}.`

## Script structure as observed
relatable problem/outcome hook -> "here's how" -> step-by-step screen demo (tap, choose, generate) narrated fast -> result shown -> free trial CTA. Brand named early (median 6.5s, ~26% in). 4 of 11 are music-only screen/UI demos (Calm, Rise, Motion 5) relying on on-screen text.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Click the link below and try out Motion for free." (Motion 11)
- "Get started with Linktree today while they still have their 7-day free trial." (Savvy Finds 7)
- "Open Remini, choose, see your future, and tap on, get your aging video." (Remini 2)

## What the strongest examples do
- Outcome first (the 'here's my setup that…' promise), then steps. Viewers see the result before the how.
- The brand is named early (median ~6.5 s) because the tool IS the content.
- Every step is a visible tap with a micro-benefit; the CTA is a free trial.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** creator holding phone to camera / pointing at phone screen, screen-recording inserts (Notion/Motion calendars, Canva editor), bedroom-at-2:15AM relatable scene (Rise), talking head + text pill (white rounded box with dark text, TikTok native style), kid/aging face morph (Remini), laptop desk setups. 9 of 11 9:16.

**Spec:** 9:16. Face-cam or hands-in-frame over a crisp screen recording (zoom 110–130% on taps). A TikTok-native white text box states the hook. Median 29.5 s (IQR 18–39 s), fast delivery (median about 250 wpm while speaking), a cut every 3–4 s. 36% of the samples are music-only UI demos with text overlays; that works for a faceless variant.

**Categories seen:** SaaS/apps: productivity (Motion), design (Canva), creator monetisation (Linktree), sleep/meditation (Calm, Rise), AI photo (Remini). MOST RELEVANT STYLE FOR AIOSCreator.

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–3s] VO: "{Outcome/pain hook}." TEXT: "{hook ≤8 words}" VISUAL: face + messy 'before' screen
[3–6s] VO: "Here's how I do it in {tool}."
[6–12s] STEP 1 VO: "First, {action}." SCREEN: {tap}
[12–18s] STEP 2 VO: "Then {action} — {micro-benefit}." SCREEN: {generate}
[18–25s] STEP 3 VO: "And {action}." SCREEN: {result}
[25–30s] VO: "{Result in numbers/time}." VISUAL: finished output
[30–33s] CTA: "{Try it free/link below}." TEXT: "{CTA} · #ad"
```
