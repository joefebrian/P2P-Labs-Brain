# Whiteboard Explainer

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A creator explains a concept by drawing or writing on a whiteboard (or a digital whiteboard), for mechanism-first education.

## When to use it
Use it for complex mechanisms (how AI script generation works, why hooks matter, funnel math), B2B and info products, and high-intent audiences. The sample is small (7 files).

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **question hook** | 0–3s | A question or number written on the board |
| 2 | **draw problem** | 3–15s | Diagram |
| 3 | **mechanism** | 15–35s | Arrows, steps |
| 4 | **product** | 35–45s | Where it fits in the diagram |
| 5 | **cta** | last 5s | Link |

## Hook formulas
- `Here's why {common approach} doesn't work. (draws it)`
- `The {N}-step {framework} every {role} should know.`
- `If you {do X}, this is what's happening. (diagram)`

## Script structure as observed
objection/question on the board -> itemised breakdown written live (cost math, mechanism diagram) -> product as the efficient answer -> offer. Median ~43s. Pairs with Comment Response (objection) and price justification.

## CTA patterns observed
- "Get 20% off Seed's DS-01 today and try it risk-free for 30 days." (Seed 2)
- "just go to [hims] and get your own personalized treatment plan" (hims 6)

## What the strongest examples do
- The board frames the question visually, so it feels educational rather than an ad.
- The drawn mechanism builds belief step by step.
- The product appears as a box in the diagram, the missing step.

## Fill-in script template
```text
[0–3s] BOARD: "{question/number}" VO: "{hook}"
[3–15s] DRAW: {problem diagram} VO: "{explain}"
[15–35s] DRAW: {mechanism steps}
[35–45s] VO: "{product} handles {step}."
[45–50s] VO: "{CTA}."
```

## Example script: AIOSCreator-type affiliate product
*Braces `{…}` = facts you must replace with true, verified details. Add the disclosure if you're an affiliate.*

**Length:** about 45 s.
- [0–3s] BOARD: "3s → 30s → $" VO: "This is the only funnel an affiliate video has."
- [3–15s] DRAW: HOOK (0–3 s) → HOLD (problem and proof) → CLICK (CTA). VO: "Lose them at 3 seconds and nothing else matters."
- [15–32s] DRAW: 3 hook boxes → 1 body → 1 CTA. VO: "So you test three hooks on the same body. That's how pros find winners."
- [32–42s] VO: "{AIOSCreator} writes the three hooks and the body for you, in whichever style fits the product." SHOT: app
- [42–45s] VO: "{Offer}. Link below. #ad"
