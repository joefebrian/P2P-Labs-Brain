# Comment Response

> Part of [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]] · Choose styles with [[04 Style Selection Guide]] · Hooks: [[02 Hook Library]] · CTAs: [[03 CTA & Offer Library]] · Generate: [[05 AIOSCreator Script Generator Spec]]

## What it is
A video reply to a (real-looking) comment, with the TikTok 'Reply to @user' sticker on screen from frame 0. It is used for objections, FAQs and social proof.

## When to use it
Use it to handle objections (price, 'does it really work', 'is it just X'), to explain a feature, or for second-touch retargeting. It is excellent for software because every FAQ becomes a video.

## Beat map
| # | Beat | Timing | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **comment sticker** | 0s | The objection or question is visible on screen and read aloud or paraphrased |
| 2 | **validate** | 1–5s | 'Fair question, I had the same fear' |
| 3 | **answer + demo** | 5–25s | Show, don't tell |
| 4 | **reframe** | 25–30s | Turn the objection into a benefit |
| 5 | **offer + cta** | last 5s | Current deal + link |

## Hook formulas
- `Reply: 'Is there actually a difference between this and {alternative}?'`
- `Reply: 'Are {product}s really that good or just hype?' → 'I've had these for {N} years.'`
- `Reply: '{too expensive}' → 'I wouldn't buy {product} either… if {condition}.'`
- `Reply: '{1-star review}' (read the negative review, then respond)`
- `If you have {condition}, do NOT get {product}. (reverse psychology)`
- `Here's what people get wrong about {product}.`

## Script structure as observed
comment sticker (objection/question) 0s -> restate/validate ("fair question") -> answer with demo/proof -> reframe objection into benefit -> offer -> CTA. Median ~36s. Works as objection handling & FAQ; also for price objections.

## CTA patterns observed
- "And right now Mudwater starter kits are 43% off..." (alecisfresh 1)
- "First time subscriptions are automatically 33% off right now" (Oats 54)
- "Right now buy one HeyShape bodysuit and get one for free." (HeyShape 6)
- "Grab it by clicking here and thank me later." (DRMTLGY 19)
- "Get yours before they sell out again." (Arrae 41)

## What the strongest examples do
- The objection is on screen before a word is spoken, so the viewer already cares about the answer.
- The best ones validate first ('fair question, I had the same fear'), then prove with a demo.
- Reverse psychology ('if you have X, do NOT get these') and reading 1-star reviews create strong curiosity.

## Visual / production spec
**Observed (keyframes reviewed):** the TikTok "Reply to @user's comment" sticker (white bubble, top-left/centre) is pinned from frame 0 in almost every video; talking head in kitchen/bathroom/office, product held to lens (Mudwater chai, razor, Oats pouches), founders at a table (harrys 13), unboxing/packing an order in response. 8+10 images = screenshot-style comment reply ads.

**Spec:** 9:16. The native reply sticker sits top-centre; talking head in a real setting; the product in hand or on screen. Median 36 s. VO about 180 wpm. Leave the sticker up at least 3 s.

**Categories seen:** food/bev, cookware, skincare, shapewear, pet, creatine, earplugs, SaaS (Canva 18 "If I had to pick just one tool to run my entire freelance business"; Motion 15).

```text
[0–4s] VO: "{Restate}. Fair question — {validation}."
[4–20s] VO: "Here's the thing: {answer}." SHOT: {demo}
[20–27s] VO: "So {reframe objection into benefit}."
[27–32s] VO: "And right now {offer}. {CTA}." TEXT: "#ad"
```
