# How to Write a UGC Script

> The master method. Part A is **derived from the 889-video study**. Part B is **external best practice** (cited). Part C is the fill-in template. Part D is the checklist. Back to [[00 UGC Playbook – Index]].

## Part A: What the data says (from this folder)
### A1. The universal skeleton
Almost every speaking video in the folder follows some version of **Hook → Problem/Context → Product reveal → Demo/Mechanism → Proof → Offer → CTA**. The styles differ in *who* delivers it and *how much* time each beat gets:

| Beat | Short ad (15–35 s) | Standard (35–60 s) | Advertorial (60–120 s+) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | 0–3 s | 0–5 s |
| Problem / agitate | 3–7 s | 3–12 s | 5–35 s (visualised symptoms, 'not your fault') |
| Reveal (product named) | 5–8 s | 8–15 s | ~40–55% of runtime |
| Demo / mechanism | 8–22 s | 15–40 s | +15–25 s (ingredient/feature → symptom mapping) |
| Proof | 22–28 s | 40–50 s | timeline ('by week 1…'), reviews, authority |
| Offer + CTA | last 3–5 s | last 5–8 s | last 10–15 s (offer stack) |

### A2. Hooks (first 3 s)
- Say it **and** show it: 84% had readable hook text in the first 3 s; the text usually restates the spoken hook in 3–8 words.
- One sentence, about 8–12 words. At the median pace of 187.5 wpm that finishes in about 3 s.
- Speak to the viewer: 33% use 'you/your' in the first 3 s; 12% open with a question.
- The most-used hook families are callout, question, negative/warning, listicle number, authority/stat, curiosity/secret, result-first, personal confession, comment reply, POV and dialogue cold-open. See [[02 Hook Library]].
- The first frame is always *doing something*: a pour, a squeeze, the product at the lens, a split-screen, a comment sticker, a POV caption. Never a logo.

### A3. Pacing
- Median 187.5 words per minute of runtime (the rate inside speech segments is higher: 213.5). Faster styles: App Walkthrough ~228.1, AI Cartoon ~169.2. Slower: Storytelling ~203.4, Testimonial ~190.2.
- A visual change about every 3 s (median 3.6 detected cuts per 10 s; B-Roll 5.0, Listicle 3.5).
- Word budget ≈ duration × 3 (for example, a 30 s ad ≈ 90 words; a 45 s ad ≈ 135 words).

### A4. Proof devices seen
Demos in real time, split-screen before/after, review counts ('thousands of 5-star reviews', 'over 50,000 5-star reviews'), sold-out claims ('sold out 3×'), credentials (CSO, skin therapist, cosmetic chemist, founder), studies (microplastics, magnesium deficiency), timelines ('around the two-week mark'), celebrity/brand association (David Beckham for IM8), and customer supercuts (Acquisition.com).

### A5. Offers & CTA
- Stack them: guarantee/risk-free (10% of videos), % off (7%), free gifts (3%), scarcity (6%), urgency words (21%).
- The close is one action verb plus one place: *'Tap the link below'*, *'Grab yours at brand.com'*, *'It's free for 7 days, so give it a shot'*.
- See [[03 CTA & Offer Library]].

### A6. Tone
First person, conversational, confessional ('I'm not proud of it, but…'), mildly irreverent ('don't be dumb'), with specific numbers. The ads that feel least like ads use humour (skits, personified characters) or authenticity cues ('Brand sent me a script and I didn't want to use it').

## Part B: External best practice (web research, cited)
- **Hook, body, close.** TikTok recommends a three-part structure (hook → body → close). It reports that 90% of ad-recall impact is captured in the first 6 s, and advises introducing the core proposition in the first 3 s. [TikTok blog](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads), [TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices)
- **Test hook variants with the body held constant.** Studying Top Ads in the Creative Center and testing 3–5 openings is common practice. [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/tiktok-ad-best-practices-improve-performance)
- **Text on screen at a readable rate.** Around 5–10 words per second, inside the safe zone. [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/tiktok-ad-best-practices-improve-performance)
- **9:16 full-screen for Reels/Stories.** Keep text and key visuals out of roughly the top 14% and bottom 35% of the frame. Show the brand and key message in the first 3 s. Design to work both muted and with sound (captions plus VO). [Meta aspect ratios](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/103816146375741), [Meta IG video best practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/188534925073536), [Hookads Reels specs](https://www.hookads.ai/blog/instagram-reels-ad-specs-2026)
- **UGC script frameworks.** Hook → Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA is the workhorse. Use PAS for problem-aware audiences, AIDA for cold introductions, and BAB for transformations. A single CTA. 15–30 s ≈ 40–80 spoken words for AI UGC. [Tagshop](https://tagshop.ai/blog/how-to-script-an-ai-ugc-video-ads-formats-and-hooks/), [inReels](https://www.inreels.ai/blog/ugc-ad-frameworks-for-tiktok-and-meta)
- **Add a social-proof beat.** Classic frameworks miss the social-proof beat that paid social needs. Spark UGC's 6-element structure is Hook → Problem & emotion → Solution → Value prop → Social proof → CTA. Suggested lengths: TikTok in-feed 15–25 s works best, YouTube Shorts 15–45 s. [Spark UGC](https://www.sparkugc.com/resources/ugc-ad-script-structure-template)
- **The hook must promise what the body delivers within about 8–12 s.** A proof stack (social, demonstrative, authority, risk reduction) closes the sale on TikTok Shop. [Moras](https://moras.ai/blog/tiktok-shop-video-script)
- **Disclosure.** Affiliate commissions and free products are material connections. Disclose in the video itself (on screen and ideally spoken), not only in the caption. Don't bury it in hashtags. [FTC Disclosures 101](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers), [FTC FAQ](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking), [16 CFR 255](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255)

**Where data and web agree:** the 3-second hook, vertical format, captions plus VO, a single CTA, and proof beats. **Where the data adds nuance:** long 60–110 s advertorials (AI Cartoon, doctor skits, RYZE storytelling) are common in this library despite the web's 15–30 s advice. Keep short cuts for cold testing and long advertorials for proven angles.

## Part C: Fill-in template (universal)
```text
TITLE: {product} · {style} · {angle}
STYLE: {one of 24}   FRAMEWORK: {HPSPC|PAS|AIDA|BAB|LISTICLE|QA_OBJECTION|STORY_ARC|DEMO_LOOP|SKIT_TWIST|MYTH_TRUTH}
LENGTH: {15|30|45|60} s   WORD BUDGET: ≈ LENGTH × 3   TALENT: {creator|founder|faceless VO|AI avatar|character}
AUDIENCE: {persona} · AWARENESS: {unaware→most aware} · MAIN PAIN: {…} · MAIN DESIRE: {…}

HOOK A ({type}): VO "{≤12 words}" | TEXT "{≤8 words}" | FIRST FRAME {motion/product/problem}
HOOK B ({type}): …
HOOK C ({type}): …

[0–3s]   HOOK      (use A/B/C)
[3–8s]   PROBLEM   VO "{specific pain + cost}"            TEXT "{…}"   SHOT {…}
[8–12s]  REVEAL    VO "Until I found / That's why {product}"  SHOT {product hero}
[12–25s] DEMO      VO "{how it works: 2–3 steps}"          SHOT {demo close-ups, cut every 2–3 s}
[25–30s] PROOF     VO "{number/review/before-after}"       TEXT "{metric}"
[30–35s] OFFER+CTA VO "{risk reversal}. {offer}. {verb} {place}."  TEXT "{OFFER} · #ad"

DISCLOSURE: VO "{I earn a commission…}" + TEXT "#ad"   CLAIMS VERIFIED: ☐
CAPTIONS: {word-by-word bold | TikTok-native box}, safe zone   MUSIC/SFX: {…}
```

## Part D: Pre-flight checklist
- [ ] Hook lands in ≤3 s on voice, text and visual
- [ ] Product named or shown by 8 s (unless advertorial style)
- [ ] Specific pain with a number or detail
- [ ] At least one proof beat
- [ ] One CTA, offer is true, deadline real
- [ ] Word count ≈ length × 3
- [ ] Captions inside the safe zone; works on mute
- [ ] Disclosure in the video (affiliate/paid)
- [ ] 3 hook variants with the same body for testing
